# Deep Reviews — sub2api provider pass 2026-09-20

Twenty review passes were run before publishing, each with a distinct lens, each required to
find something real or state what would have made it fire. Fixes were folded into the
report before the next pass. Findings are numbered; the disposition is recorded.

Method note: the report is revision 1, so "how many claims a previous revision got wrong"
has no answer. What follows is the closest substitute — the errors this revision caught in
itself before shipping.

---

## Pass 1 — Re-derivation (recompute every multiple from the raw snapshot)

Re-derived from the archived rate cards, not from the report text: 0.15× (hao.ai 1.5/10 and
7.5/50), 0.167× (LanoX 1.67/10, 8.35/50), 0.03× (CodexEverywhere 0.30/10, 1.50/50), 0.045×
(Kiro 0.225/5, 1.125/25), 0.24× (Max 2.40/10, 12/50), 0.40× (OpenModel 4/10, 20/50), 2.1×
(CCTK 10.5/5, 52.5/25).
**Finding 1 — none.** Every multiple in `data/rate-cards.csv` re-derives exactly from the
snapshot. What would have made it fire: any quotient differing from the printed label by
more than rounding.

## Pass 2 — Claim-scope (VERIFIED / ADVERTISED / UNKNOWN)

Audited every price sentence against whether a public card existed. Found that the first
draft's Ranking 1 header said "cheapest *published* token rates" but the table mixed 5
verified rows with 3 advertisement-only rows under continuous numbering 1–8.
**Finding 2 — FIXED.** Table split into Part A (VERIFIED, ranked by printed multiple) and
Part B (ADVERTISED, below every verified row), so an advertisement can no longer outrank a
checkable price.

## Pass 3 — Consistency router (report ↔ CSVs ↔ sources)

Cross-checked the report against `providers-database.csv`, `rate-cards.csv`,
`subscription-plans.csv` and `sources/`.
**Finding 3 — FIXED.** Ranking 2 ordered by capability (Qiniu first) while the ranking is
"cheapest plans"; reordered ascending by price so AIGoCode Pro (CNY 399) precedes Qiniu
Enterprise S (CNY 2,999), matching `subscription-plans.csv`.
**Finding 4 — FIXED.** Report prose claimed "6 of 17 AI providers publish rate cards" was
also stated as "5"; reconciled to five *token* rate cards (CodexEverywhere, hao.ai, LanoX,
OpenModel, CCTK) with the credit/per-image cards listed separately.

## Pass 4 — Adversarial / security

Read as an attacker and a compliance reviewer. No credential, key or session appears in
the pass. Ran the repository's `check-no-secrets.sh` (default rules) against the whole tree:
zero findings after the fix below; `--public` rules: zero on this pass.
**Finding 5 — FIXED.** The first scan fired on two vendor **documentation placeholders** of
the form `http://user:pass@host` (literally `username_custom_zone_US` / `password`) inside
snapshotted pages. They are not live credentials, but the shape is exactly what the scanner
exists to catch and the raw HTML also tripped the public-fingerprint rules on unrelated
vendor emails and asset hashes. Fix: the raw HTML was replaced by text extracts (the
2026-09-13 precedent) and the placeholder/email/path/hash shapes were redacted, as recorded
in `sources/README.md`. 
**Finding 6 — confirmed risk disclosed, not fixed (correctly).** The subject's own README
carries an explicit ToS-risk notice, and the entire 0.03×–0.4× band is consumer-quota resale.
The report states this at the top rather than burying it. Prompt-injection check: scraped
pages were parsed as data, never executed; no instruction in any fetched page was followed.

## Pass 7 — Ranking-method audit

Asked whether the ranking answers the operator's question ("rank all providers by the
cheapest plans") rather than a question of the reviewer's choosing.
**Finding 9 — FIXED.** Ranking 1 covers token rates; Ranking 2 covers flat plans; proxies are

## Pass 8 — Advertisement-vs-page cross-check

Compared every sponsor blurb in the subject README against the provider's own current page.
**Finding 10 — NEW, recorded.** APIMart: README says "GPT-Image-2 from $0.006 per image, 160+
images per dollar"; APIMart's `/model` page lists **$0.0085 before 20% off**. ColaProxy:
README says "as low as $0.3/GB"; `colaproxy.com/pricing` says **$1.6/GB**. Both added to the
misdirection section as evidence that sponsor copy is not a price.

## Pass 9 — Arithmetic-in-advertisement audit

Checked the internal arithmetic of each sponsor's discount claim.
**Finding 11 — NEW, recorded.** PP.dog states "combined rate multiplier as low as 0.03x, just
0.35% of official pricing" (0.03× = 3%, not 0.35%). PPToken states "0.16x rate multiplier,
with overall cost at roughly 2.2% of official pricing" (0.16× = 16%, not 2.2%). Neither pair
can both be true; both are flagged. They remain ADVERTISED, not promoted to a ranking.

## Pass 10 — Multiplier-direction audit

Checked the sign/direction of every multiplier, because a resale can price *above* official.
**Finding 12 — NEW, recorded.** CCTK's Claude group multiplier is **×2.1**, i.e. dearer than
official, while its marketing says "a fraction of the official cost". The GPT-Pro group is
0.6×. Recorded prominently; this is the single most decision-relevant reversal in the pass.

## Pass 11 — Long-context-tier audit

Checked whether a headline multiple survives the prompt sizes a coding agent actually sends.
**Finding 13 — recorded.** LanoX bills >272K at 2× ($3.34/$12.525 on Astra); CodexEverywhere
documents GPT 1.5×–2× above 272K, Claude 1h cache write 2× and 5m 1.25×. A 0.167× headline is
therefore an entry-tier number, not a whole-workload number. Stated in the 1M-context table.

## Pass 12 — "Free" audit

Tested the strong claim in the operator's brief ("best free providers").
**Finding 14 — recorded, and it constrains the answer.** No advertised provider offers a
genuine ongoing free frontier tier. LanoX's "NVIDIA free / 500+ free models", Qiniu's
3M/12M-token grant, CodexEverywhere's $20 and Pateway's $1+$3 are trials or marketing credits.
The report says this plainly instead of crowning a "best free provider".

## Pass 13 — Reliability-claim audit

Separated *paper* reliability from measured reliability.
**Finding 15 — recorded.** CCTK's marketing page states **98.9% platform uptime** while its
SLA section states a **99.9% monthly target** — a 1.0-point gap between target and headline.
LanoX claims 99.9% channel availability. Neither was measured here; both are cited as claims.
AIGoCode's "99.9% service stability" is likewise a marketing figure with no measurement.

## Pass 14 — Currency and comparability audit

**Finding 16 — FIXED.** No exchange rate is applied anywhere; CNY plans are ranked natively,

## Pass 15 — Measurement-validity audit

provisioned" for the 798-byte Aimzoon shell.

## Pass 19 — The first-explanation trap

misdirection, rather than only praising the winner, is the point of the section.

## Pass 21 — Scope audit (self-correction)

**Finding 23 — FIXED.** Passes 1–20 treated the 26-row README sponsor table as "the provider
universe". It is a sponsorship list. The code supports ten first-class platforms
(`domain/constants.go:20-35`), six account types including a BYO-base-url `upstream` type

code-grounded surface and three new datasets, and reframed this README as the sponsor-market
ranking only. The error was one of *scope*, not arithmetic: every sponsor-table number was

**Finding 24 — recorded.** `assets/partners/logos/` holds 32 files but the sponsor tables in

shallow so there is no removal history. The sponsor list is therefore not even the complete

**Also recorded.** Tracker-verified but **not** in the pinned tree (open PRs, so not

## Pass 22 — Second subject, and does the tooling generalize?

Applied the same method to `router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI` and asked whether the process
scales without hand-reading.
**Finding 25 — FIXED (new subject).** CLIProxyAPI's README advertises 5 providers
(`README.md:14-40`) while the code supports 8 auth providers
(`internal/auth/`) across 13 registry tiers and 135 models
(`internal/registry/models/models.json`). Same error class as Finding 23, now
covered by the reconciled report.
**Finding 26 — FIXED (tooling).** Two hand passes in a row missed providers because each
relied on one artifact. Fix: `tools/mine-providers.py` extracts providers from eight
independent signals (dirs, consts, switch labels, JSON registries, YAML, markdown
tables, markdown link-hosts, vocabulary) and labels each row with a confidence;
`tools/research-providers.sh` runs it over N repos and `reconcile-providers.py` merges
them into `providers-union.csv`. A layout change no longer loses a provider silently.
**Finding 27 — recorded.** The vocabulary fallback initially matched ordinary English
("inference", "together") and library names ("litellm"), producing false providers.
Fixed by requiring a quoted token, a hyphen, or membership in a curated strong set;
the evaluation note is left in the script docstring.
**Finding 28 — recorded (cross-repo).** The same vendor advertises different prices on
the two sponsor pages (RapidProxy $0.65 vs $0.55/GB). Sponsor copy is not a price list
for either project.

### Pass 23 — the category-enumeration pass (2026-09-20, revision 2)
**Finding 29 — FIXED (method, this is the important one).** Passes 1–22 established
presence by searching for a name and inferred absence from a null result. That is
invalid: absence requires enumerating the category and checking each member. Prompted by
external review naming **Command Code** — a coding agent for open models, 100K+
developers claimed — the check was redone by enumerating the agent market (GitHub search
API, 214 repos) and cross-checking every member. Command Code and Open Interpreter were
absent from all three sources; a literal search had merely confirmed that the earlier
passes never wrote them down. Fix: `PROVIDER-BY-PROVIDER.md` +
`data/agents-universe.csv`, which state the category and the per-member result.
**Finding 30 — FIXED (false-positive guard).** The first cross-check matched a bare
`cursor`/`continue`/`amp`; `cursor` has 136 hits in sub2api (all pagination) and
`continue` 557 (the Go keyword). Left uncorrected this would have reported dozens of
phantom agent integrations. Fixed by requiring product-context patterns
(`cursor\.(sh|com)`, `continuedev`, `ampcode`); the raw counts are recorded so a reader
can see the difference.
**Finding 31 — recorded (scope).** A category enumeration is still not a census. An
agent with no GitHub presence, no vendor page and no source mention remains invisible to
this method. The 18 absences in `data/agents-universe.csv` are "not in these three
sources", not "does not exist".
