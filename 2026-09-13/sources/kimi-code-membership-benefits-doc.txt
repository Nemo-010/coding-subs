Requests from the CLI, VS Code, and third-party tools all count toward that quota.See the Kimi membership page for tiers and pricing.Quota and Limits ​Kimi Code&#39;s quota refreshes automatically every 7 days from your subscription date; unused quota does not roll over. Beyond the weekly quota, there is also a rolling 5-hour rate window — even with quota remaining, too many requests in a short time trigger rate limiting, which recovers automatically once the window rolls over.All logged-in devices and API Keys share the same quota; devices inactive for over 30 days are automatically unbound and can be restored by running /login again. Log in to the Kimi Code Console at any time to check remaining quota and rate-limit status and to manage API Keys and devices.Kimi Code shares quota with your Kimi membership plan: if your Kimi membership&#39;s monthly total is reached, Kimi Code quota is frozen until the monthly quota resets or you upgrade. Monthly usage can be viewed on the Subscription page.Extra Usage ​When your subscription quota runs out, you can keep making requests using your Extra Usage balance without waiting for a refresh. Only subscribed members can enable it, and Kimi on the web and Kimi Code share the same Extra Usage balance. See the full terms in the Extra Usage Agreement.Top-up entry points ​From the main site: go to the Kimi Subscription page, scroll to the &quot;Extra Usage&quot; module at the bottom then choose an amount and complete payment.From the Kimi Code Console: once your weekly quota or 5-hour quota reaches over 80% used, the progress card shows a Buy Extra Usage button that takes you to the subscription page to purchase.Enabling and managing ​Turn on the &quot;Extra Usage&quot; toggle, and when your subscription quota runs out, the system automatically deducts from your Extra Usage balance. Click Settings in the module to enable a monthly spending cap and set the maximum you can spend this month; the progress bar shows this month&#39;s Extra Usage consumption in real time.Billing and rules ​Billing: charged by actual usage — the more content read and generated, and the more complex the task, the higher the cost; the balance is shown in RMB, with rates as displayed on the platform. Extra Usage pricing is close to the official API pricing of the Kimi Open Platform; for heavy usage, upgrading your subscription is usually more cost-effective.Deduction priority: time-limited quotas such as the subscription are deducted first; Extra Usage is deducted last as a fallback. Once enabled, even if your subscription quota is maxed out, Extra Usage is consumed seamlessly and tasks don&#39;t stop or error out.Spending cap: enable a &quot;monthly spending cap&quot; in settings to limit how much Extra Usage can be spent this month; without it, there is no cap.Validity and stacking: the balance does not expire and stacks across top-ups; it remains usable after the subscription lapses, but you can&#39;t top up during the lapse — re-subscribe first to top up again.Refunds: generally non-refundable, non-transferable, and non-redeemable.Billing example (from official examples, for a rough sense of scale; actual rates per the platform): a simple request (e.g. &quot;recommend a rock song&quot;) costs about ¥0.03; a complex multi-step task (e.g. &quot;plan a week-long road trip from Chengdu to western Sichuan&quot;) costs about ¥1.6.Top-up limits: minimum ¥25 per top-up, up to 10 times and ¥3,000 per day, with a balance cap of ¥10,000.Checking your usage ​Kimi web / app: Settings → Subscription &amp; Invoices → My Quota.Kimi Code Console: once Extra Usage is enabled, log in to the Console to view your balance and usage.Kimi Code CLI: type /usage on the command line to check directly.FAQ ​Do Kimi on the web and Kimi Code use the same Extra Usage wallet? Yes. The Extra Usage balance is shared between Kimi and Kimi Code — top up once and use it in both; the Extra Usage you top up under &quot;My Quota&quot; and the one in the Kimi Code Console are the same balance.Which is deducted first, Extra Usage or subscription quota? Time-limited quotas such as the subscription are deducted first; Extra Usage is only deducted as a fallback once all time-limited quotas are exhausted.When I hit the monthly / weekly / hourly limit, or get rate-limited mid-session, can I keep going? Yes. Once Extra Usage is enabled you&#39;re not affected by subscription limits — whenever any quota is reached it seamlessly switches to the Extra Usage balance, so tasks don&#39;t stop or error out, and only Extra Usage is deducted without affecting your membership quota refresh.How is Extra Usage billed, and how does it compare with a same-priced subscription or Open Platform API? Billing depends on the request&#39;s input/output volume and cache hit rate; Extra Usage pricing is close to the Kimi Open Platform&#39;s official API, and for heavy usage upgrading your subscription is usually more cost-effective.Can unused balance roll over to next month, and does it stack? Yes — it&#39;s credit-based, doesn&#39;t expire, and stacks; it stays usable after the subscription lapses, but you can&#39;t top up during the lapse and must re-subscribe to top up again.When the balance is nearly used up (e.g. a 25 RMB top-up), will the current request finish? It guarantees the &quot;last model call&quot; completes even if its billing exceeds the limit, but it doesn&#39;t guarantee the whole task will finish.Is Extra Usage available for Enterprise? Not yet supported.After I close my account, is the balance refunded?
